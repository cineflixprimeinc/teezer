FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I get started with Teezer Ads? +

Create a free account, upload or build a video in the studio, set your audience and goal, and launch. Your first campaign can be live in minutes.

How precise is the audience targeting? +

Target by age, gender, location, language, interests, and on-platform behavior. Our engine optimizes delivery toward the people most likely to convert.

Do I need to create the videos myself? +

No. Use the built-in creator studio, tap our creator marketplace, or upload your own assets — whatever fits your workflow.

How do I measure results? +

A real-time dashboard tracks views, watch-time, engagement, and conversions per campaign, with export and API access available.

Is my brand and data safe? +

Teezer is SOC 2 Type II compliant with brand-safety controls, granular permissions, and encryption in transit and at rest.
